# Third-party artwork

The dinosaur sprite sheets embedded in `index.html` originate from the Chromium offline dinosaur game, as distributed in the [Soheilexe/Chrome-Dino](https://github.com/Soheilexe/Chrome-Dino) standalone adaptation. The 1× and 2× sprite coordinates are taken from Chromium's `offline_sprite_definitions.ts`.
